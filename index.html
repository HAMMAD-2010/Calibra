A careful scientific approach

The voice prototype measures acoustic features such as pitch (F0), loudness and pauses, and keeps them separate from anything you report about how you feel. Voice alone cannot reliably tell whether someone is happy, stressed or unwell: language, accent, age, microphone and background noise all matter.

There is no trained emotion model in this prototype, so it never outputs an emotional label or a diagnosis.

Method notes

F0 is estimated per 40 ms frame by autocorrelation over 75–400 Hz, on frames with enough energy and a clear periodic peak. Loudness is relative to digital full scale (dBFS), not calibrated sound pressure. The signal-to-noise figure is a rough estimate from the quietest frames. Short or noisy clips give unreliable values, and the tool says so.

Privacy & limits

Voice

Analyzed locally in your browser. Not uploaded. Discard removes the clip immediately.

Chat

Messages are sent to Calibra’s backend (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards them to Google’s Gemini API to generate a reply. This page stores nothing. What the backend and provider log or keep depends on their own settings, so don’t promise confidentiality until that is documented.

AI limits

The assistant can be wrong, can’t diagnose, and can’t handle emergencies.
