Understand the Situation. Explore Your Next Step.

Calibra is a structured reflection and support-navigation tool. It uses fictional scenarios to help you separate what is known from what is assumed, consider reasonable options, and find people and resources who can help.

Explore a Scenario How It Works

Three steps

1Choose a Scenario

Pick one of four fictional situations between students. No personal details are needed.

2Reflect

Work through what happened, what might be an assumption, and a few possible responses, one short step at a time.

3Explore Next Steps

Review options such as pausing, considering a respectful boundary, or speaking with a trusted adult.

What Calibra Can and Cannot Do

Calibra can

  • Help you slow down and separate observable facts from assumptions.
  • Show reasonable options without telling you which one to choose.
  • Point you toward trusted adults and support resources.
  • Let you leave at any time with the Exit button.

Calibra cannot

  • Diagnose mental-health conditions or assess how you feel.
  • Provide therapy, counselling, or medical advice.
  • Replace a trusted adult, teacher, counselor, or qualified professional.
  • Provide emergency help. If you are in immediate danger, contact a trusted adult and local emergency services.
  • Guarantee that any option will solve a situation.
Prototype notice: This is a student research prototype. It has not yet been shown to be effective, and it does not prevent bullying or improve mental health.
