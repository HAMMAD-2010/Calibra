Research Assessment Demo

Participant use disabled Demo mode

Not for real participants. This section is for the student research team. It must not be used by students until the supervising teacher and the relevant review committee have approved the research protocol. No responses are collected, saved, or sent.

Purpose of this assessment

The assessment would ask questions about a fictional scenario and how a person might respond. It is a research instrument, not a test, a personality measure, or an assessment of mental health. Responses would be used only for the study, as described in the approved protocol.

Demo questions

These sample questions use the fictional "Hurtful Comment" scenario. They are placeholders written by the research team and are not an approved instrument.

Fictional scenario: A classmate made a comment about another student's appearance at lunch. Some people nearby laughed.

Q1. Which of these is directly stated in the scenario?
Q2. Which next step would you explore first?

Selecting this clears the answers on this page without sending anything.

Scoring rubric

Rubric pending review. No scoring criteria have been approved. Scores are not calculated in this demo, and the rubric will not be modified automatically. The research team must configure and review the rubric before any use.

What happens to responses

In this demo, answers are kept only in the open page's memory and are cleared when you leave the page. Nothing is sent to a server, saved to local storage, or linked to a name. A website checkbox alone is not treated as informed consent. Real studies would require a separate consent process approved by the review committee.
