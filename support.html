Support Resources

You don't have to handle a difficult situation alone. These are ways to get help from people who can support you.

Calibra is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get help straight away from a trusted adult and local emergency services.
Verification status: No contact details or links have been verified by the project team yet. Every entry below is a placeholder and must not be used as a real contact until it is confirmed.

1. Trusted adults

A trusted adult could be a parent or guardian, a teacher, a school counselor, or another adult you feel safe approaching. You can start with one sentence, such as "I'd like some advice about something that happened." You do not need to share everything at once.
