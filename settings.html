Accessibility and Settings

Adjust how Calibra looks while you use it. These settings apply only to the page you have open. They are not saved, so they reset when you move to another page or reload.

Display options

Increases text size across the site.

Uses stronger text and border colours.

Turns off gentle animations. Your system's reduced-motion setting is also respected.

Keyboard and screen reader use

  • Press Tab to move between links and buttons. Focus is shown with a dark outline.
  • Press Enter or Space to choose a button or answer card.
  • Use the skip link at the top of each page to jump to the main content.
  • Form errors are announced in plain language.

Leaving Calibra

The Exit button is available on every page. It takes you away from Calibra immediately. Nothing is stored, so nothing needs to be cleared.
