About the Research

Status: planning stage. Participant recruitment, surveys, testing, and data collection have not started and will not begin until all required school, supervisor, and ethics approvals have been obtained. This project has not yet established whether Calibra is effective.

1. Project Overview

Calibra is a student research prototype. It is a non-diagnostic, psychoeducational reflection and support-navigation tool for secondary-school students. It uses fictional peer-interaction scenarios.

2. The Problem Being Investigated

Students sometimes face upsetting social situations and may be unsure how to think them through or who to speak with. The project asks whether a structured reflection pathway can help students think about these situations more thoughtfully, compared with a standard information page.

3. Research Question

Does a structured digital reflection tool help secondary-school students select appropriate support options in a fictional upsetting peer-interaction scenario, compared with a standard information page?

4. Hypothesis

The project team has proposed a hypothesis, which will be finalised with the supervising teacher before any study begins. It is a proposal to be tested, not a finding. No result has been shown.

5. Planned Experimental Comparison

The planned comparison is between a group using Calibra's structured reflection pathway and a comparison group reading a standard information page. Both groups would respond to the same fictional scenarios. The design, group allocation, and procedures must be approved before use.

6. Primary Outcome Measure

The primary outcome is a score based on a predefined, scenario-based rubric. The rubric has not yet been finalised. It must be reviewed by the supervising teacher and relevant reviewers before use, and it will not be applied to any participant until approved.

7. Limitations

  • The scenarios are fictional and may not reflect real situations.
  • Scores on a scenario-based rubric measure a response to a fictional case, not a student's wellbeing or ability to handle real situations.
  • Any comparison would be limited by sample size, setting, and the short time frame of the study.
  • Calibra does not diagnose, assess mental health, or prevent bullying, and this project does not test those outcomes.

8. Ethics and Privacy

  • No ethics approval has been granted at this time. Required school, supervisor, and ethics approvals must be obtained before any participant contact.
  • Participation would be voluntary, and a website checkbox would not be treated as informed consent on its own.
  • The study is designed not to require participants to describe personal or traumatic experiences.
  • Participants would be able to withdraw before submission.
  • Data collection, if approved, would avoid names and unnecessary identifiers. The current prototype does not store or transmit responses.
