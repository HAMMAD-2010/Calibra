How Calibra Works

Calibra is a structured reflection tool. It guides you through a fictional situation one small step at a time, so you can slow down and look at what is known before deciding what to do.

The reflection process

  1. 1

    What happened?

    Identify which details are directly stated in the scenario.

  2. 2

    What might be an assumption?

    Notice where a guess about another person's thoughts or intentions has crept in.

  3. 3

    What are some possible responses?

    Look at two or three reasonable, non-judgmental options.

  4. 4

    What could be a safe next step?

    Explore options such as pausing, a respectful boundary when it feels safe, or speaking with a trusted adult.

What to expect

Choose from options

Answers are selected from predefined cards, so you never have to write about a personal experience.

Change your mind

Go back, change an answer, or skip a step. There is no right score.

Exit at any time

Use the Exit button on any page to leave the experience straight away.

Limitations

Calibra is not therapy, diagnosis, or emergency support. It does not assess your emotions or mental health, and it cannot guarantee any outcome. For personal concerns, speak with a trusted adult or a qualified professional. See Support Resources.
